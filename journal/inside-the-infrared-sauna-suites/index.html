Inside the infrared sauna suites

We have two private infrared suites, one for a single guest and a larger one that seats two. Each is wood-panelled and pre-heated before you arrive, with lighting you can set to a colour you like. Sessions are 45 minutes and the door is yours to open whenever you want a break.

Bring water and drink some before and after. Wear what you are comfortable in, or a towel and the robe and slippers we provide, and leave jewellery and phones in the locker. At the end, a chilled eucalyptus towel is waiting.

If you would like to make an afternoon of it, the sauna pairs with our Salt & Sage Body Scrub or with the Signature Glow Facial as a combo. If you have a medical condition or are pregnant, check with your own doctor before booking any heat treatment.
