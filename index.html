Our Story

Rooted in care.
Grounded in expertise.

Serenity & Stone was founded in 2012 by a team of licensed therapists who believed that a spa should feel both professional and deeply human. We opened our doors in Brooklyn with six treatment rooms, a commitment to using sustainably sourced products, and a single guiding principle: every guest leaves feeling more themselves.

Over twelve years, we have grown to seven suites, two private infrared sauna rooms, and a team of fifteen therapists and estheticians, each with at least five years of professional experience.

Every suite is cleaned and reset between guests, to the hygiene and safety standards our therapists trained under.
