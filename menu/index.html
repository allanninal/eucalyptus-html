◈ Good to know

  • Arrival

    Please arrive 15 minutes before your treatment, to change, sip some tea and settle in.

  • Cancellation

    Please give us at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or move a booking. Inside 24 hours we may charge half the treatment price.

  • Late arrival

    We end on time so the next guest is not kept waiting, so a late arrival means a shorter treatment at the full price.

  • Minimum age

    Guests must be 16 or over. Under 18s need a parent or guardian to book and to sign for them.

  • What to wear

    Robes and slippers are ours. Wear what is comfortable to arrive in; you are always covered with a towel or sheet, and only the area being treated is uncovered.

  • Health and safety

    The notes on each treatment are general and are not medical advice. Tell us about pregnancy, injuries, allergies, medicines and health conditions when you book, and again at your consultation. If you are unsure, ask your doctor before you book. We may change or decline a treatment to keep you safe. If you feel unwell during a treatment, tell your therapist at once.
